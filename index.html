I am a mathematician at the University of Cambridge, working in mathematical statistics and the analysis of partial differential equations.

My research concerns Bayesian approaches to non-linear inverse problems: recovering an unknown parameter of a PDE, such as the diffusivity of a medium or the viscosity of a fluid, from noisy measurements of its solution, and proving that these methods recover the true parameter as more data arrive.

I have also worked on problems in analytic number theory and additive combinatorics through the SMALL REU at Williams College.
