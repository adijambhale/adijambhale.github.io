Research

I work in mathematical statistics, with a focus on Bayesian methods for non-linear inverse problems governed by partial differential equations.

Papers

  • 2026

    Posterior consistency under dependent errors in non-linear inverse problems with applications to time evolution equations

    Preprint, 2026.

    • arXiv:2610.07240
    • PDF
    Summary

    Gives conditions for the posterior distribution of a PDE parameter to concentrate around the truth when the measurement noise is correlated in time, as it is for real sensors. The examples are the Navier–Stokes equations with unknown viscosity and initial condition, and a reaction–diffusion equation with spatially varying diffusivity, where new stability estimates for a Darcy-type transport operator are needed.

  • 2025

    On low frequency inference for diffusions without the hot spots conjecture

    with Giovanni S. Alberti, Douglas Barnes and Richard Nickl

    Mathematical Statistics and Learning 8 (2025), no. 3/4, 305–322.

    • Journal (open access)
    • arXiv:2410.19393
    Summary

    Two main results of Nickl (2024) on recovering the diffusivity of a reflected diffusion from discretely sampled positions relied on the hot spots conjecture from spectral geometry, which remains open. We remove that dependence: we characterise minimax rates for estimating the transition operator on any smooth convex domain, and prove a Lipschitz stability estimate for recovering the diffusivity from it.
